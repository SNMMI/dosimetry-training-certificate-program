# Code of Conduct

This community is intended to provide a respectful, professional, and constructive learning environment.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and collegial
- Assume good intent
- Keep discussion constructive and educational
- Recognize the multidisciplinary nature of the community
- Avoid sharing patient-identifiable, confidential, or restricted information

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or disrespectful language
- Sharing patient-identifiable information
- Sharing confidential institutional or trial information
- Posting exam content or restricted course materials
- Spam or unrelated promotional content

Moderators may remove content or restrict participation when needed to maintain a safe and professional learning environment.
