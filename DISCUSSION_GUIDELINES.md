# Discussion Guidelines

This community is intended to support professional learning and discussion related to the SNMMI Dosimetry Training and Certificate Program.

## Good Discussion Questions

A good question includes:

- The course module or topic
- A clear description of the question
- What you already reviewed or tried
- Relevant technical or clinical context
- No patient-identifiable information

## Example

**Course module or topic:** Time-activity curves  
**Question:** In what situations would a monoexponential fit be inappropriate for estimating time-integrated activity?  
**What I already reviewed or tried:** I reviewed the course section on TAC fitting but am unsure how to handle uptake-phase data.  
**Relevant context:** This is a conceptual question, not a patient-specific question.  
**Type of question:** Conceptual / technical

## Please Avoid

- Posting patient-identifiable information
- Posting exam questions or answers
- Uploading copyrighted course materials
- Asking for medical advice about a specific patient
- Sharing confidential institutional documents
- Posting unpublished trial data
