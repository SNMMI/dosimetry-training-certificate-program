# Privacy and PHI Policy

This community is for education and professional discussion. It must not be used to share patient-identifiable information or protected health information.

Do not post:

- Patient names
- Dates of birth
- Medical record numbers
- Accession numbers
- Clinical screenshots with identifiers
- DICOM images or reports containing identifiers
- Institution-specific confidential documents
- Information that could reasonably identify a patient
- Unpublished clinical trial data

Case-based questions are welcome only when fully de-identified and framed for educational discussion.

When in doubt, generalize the question.

Moderators may remove or edit posts that include patient-identifiable information, confidential information, or restricted course material.
