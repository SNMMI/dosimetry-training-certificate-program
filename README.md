# SNMMI Dosimetry Training and Certificate Program

Welcome to the community discussion space for the SNMMI Dosimetry Training and Certificate Program.

This repository supports course-related discussion, participant Q&A, implementation conversations, resource sharing, and professional learning in radiopharmaceutical therapy dosimetry.

## Purpose

This space is intended to support:

- Questions about course concepts and modules
- Discussion of radiopharmaceutical therapy dosimetry methods
- Clinical implementation questions
- Technical and software-related discussion
- Sharing of public resources, guidelines, and references
- Continued professional learning beyond the formal course platform

## Important Boundaries

Please do not post:

- Patient-identifiable information or protected health information
- Screenshots from clinical systems
- DICOM images or reports containing identifiers
- Confidential institutional documents or workflows
- Unpublished clinical trial data
- Exam questions, exam answers, or assessment content
- Copyrighted course slides, recordings, or restricted SNMMI course materials

Case-based discussion is welcome only when fully de-identified and framed for educational discussion.

This community does not provide patient-specific medical advice. Questions should be framed for education, implementation, or general professional discussion.

## How to Participate

Use the **Discussions** tab to ask questions, respond to others, and participate in course-related conversations.

You do not need programming experience to participate. For this course community, GitHub is being used as a structured discussion forum.

## Suggested Question Format

When asking a question, please include:

- Course module or topic
- Your question
- What you already reviewed or tried
- Relevant context, without patient-identifiable information
- Whether the question is conceptual, clinical, technical, software-related, or implementation-focused

## Community Expectations

Please keep discussions professional, respectful, constructive, and educational. This community is intended to support learners across disciplines, including physicians, physicists, technologists, radiopharmacists, researchers, trainees, educators, and industry colleagues.

## Maintainers

This community is maintained by the SNMMI Dosimetry Training and Certificate Program team.
